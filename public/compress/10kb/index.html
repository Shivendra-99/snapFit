Frequently asked questions

How do I get my photograph under the 10 KB size limit set by NEET, UGC NET, MPSC, GATE, CAT, BPSC, TNPSC or NDA?

Upload the picture here and move the quality slider down until the preview shows the file weighing less than 10 KB. The whole process runs in your browser, so the image never leaves your device. You then save the compressed result and upload it to the examination portal.

Does the tool upload my photograph to any server while compressing it?

No. Compression happens entirely within your browser using JavaScript, so nothing is sent across the internet. Your original picture stays on your computer, and only you decide where the smaller version goes.

Why does the photograph sometimes look blurry after compression?

JPEG is a lossy format, so reducing file size means discarding some picture detail. The live preview lets you balance clarity against size, and you should keep the quality slider as high as the 10 KB ceiling allows.
