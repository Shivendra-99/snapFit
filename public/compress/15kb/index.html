Frequently asked questions

How small must my photograph be for these government and entrance examinations?

Most recruitment and entrance bodies, such as NEET, UGC NET, MPSC, GATE, CAT, BPSC, TNPSC, RRB and NDA, cap the uploaded image at 15 KB. Our compressor lets you watch the file size tick downwards as JPEG quality is dialled back, so you can land safely below that threshold without guesswork.

Does my picture ever leave the device while it is being compressed?

No. The resizing and quality adjustments happen entirely inside your browser. Nothing is transmitted to a server, so a passport-style image or signature stays private, and there is no account, login or payment involved.

Which formats are accepted, and what comes out at the other end?

You can drop in a JPG, PNG or WebP file. The tool saves the result as a JPEG because that format hits the smallest size for photographs of this kind while still keeping faces recognisable, which matters for hall tickets and admit cards.

Will reducing the quality make my face blur beyond recognition?

The slider moves in fine steps, and the preview updates with each change, so you can stop the moment the file drops under 15 KB while the features, ears and background still look clean. If the picture starts to smear, simply start again with a tighter crop before compressing.
