Frequently asked questions

How does the tool bring a photo below 200 KB?

It re-encodes the image as JPEG and lowers the quality factor in small steps, re-saving after each step. A live preview updates as the slider moves, and processing stops the moment the file size drops under 200 KB.

Is it safe to use for NEET, GATE, UPSC or visa photos?

Yes. Nothing leaves your device, so the image is not uploaded to any server. This also means there is no queue, no waiting, and no account needed. You get the final compressed photo straight from your own browser.

Will lowering JPEG quality make my photo look bad?

For small head-and-shoulders photos the difference between quality 95 and quality 70 is usually hard to spot, especially at the reduced file size needed for portals. The tool lets you eyeball the result in the preview before you download, so you can trade clarity for size only as far as you are comfortable.

Does it work on a phone, and do I need an app?

It works in any modern mobile browser, including on Android and iPhone, so there is nothing to install. You pick the file from your gallery, watch the size shrink in the preview, and save the compressed image back to your phone.
