How to resize your exam signature

  1. Sign on plain white paper and photograph it.
  2. Open SnapFit, pick your exam and switch to Signature mode.
  3. Download the correctly sized JPEG and upload it to the form.

Frequently asked questions

What is the signature size for exam forms?

Most Indian exam forms want a signature scanned at roughly 140–200 px wide by 60–100 px tall, saved as a JPEG between 4 KB and 50 KB on a white background. Exact numbers vary by exam — see the table above.

How do I resize my signature for an exam form?

Sign on white paper, photograph it, open SnapFit, choose your exam and switch to Signature mode. SnapFit crops and compresses the signature to that exam's required size.

Is the signature tool free and private?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser at no cost, and your signature image is never uploaded anywhere.
