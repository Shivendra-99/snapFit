Frequently asked questions

How do I resize a photo to exact pixels?

Open SnapFit's Custom preset, enter the width and height in pixels, and upload your photo. It is resized to exactly those dimensions, and you can also cap the file size in KB.

How do I resize a photo in cm?

Decide the print size in centimetres and convert to pixels — at 300 DPI, 1 cm is about 118 px. Enter those pixel values in the Custom preset. A common ID size of 3.5 × 4.5 cm maps to roughly 413 × 531 px.

Will resizing to exact pixels stretch my photo?

SnapFit fits your photo to the frame and centres it, so faces are not stretched. You can nudge the zoom and vertical position to frame it correctly.

Is it free and private?

Yes — free, no account, and every image is processed on your device.
