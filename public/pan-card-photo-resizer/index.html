Frequently asked questions

What photo size is needed for a PAN card?

PAN card applications generally ask for a recent passport-style colour photograph, about 3.5 × 2.5 cm, on a plain light or white background with the full face visible. Online forms usually want a small JPEG, often around 20–50 KB. Always confirm the exact figure on the NSDL / UTIITSL portal you are using.
